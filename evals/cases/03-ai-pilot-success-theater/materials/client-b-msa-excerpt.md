# Client B — Master Services Agreement (excerpt)

**§9.4 Use of Client Materials.** Supplier shall use Client Materials solely to perform the Services. Supplier shall not use Client Materials, in whole or in part, to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or otherwise improve any machine learning model, whether for Client or for any third party, without Client's prior written consent.

**§9.5 Automated Processing.** Any material change to the method by which Services are performed, including the introduction of automated decision-making affecting catalog data published to Client channels, requires thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Client.
