Quick feedback on the tagging pilot since you asked:

- The team still reviews every tag before publishing. Nobody is comfortable skipping review yet.
- For basic apparel it's pretty good. For footwear and accessories we fix a lot — materials and closure types especially.
- Does it save time? Probably some on apparel. Hard to say overall; we haven't measured. Before the pilot we averaged ~4.5 minutes per product for manual tagging (from our Q1 time study).
- 18 people have logins, but in practice 5–6 of us use the review UI daily; the rest still tag in the old spreadsheet flow.
- Autumn/winter catalog starts arriving in October — very different product mix (outerwear, boots).
